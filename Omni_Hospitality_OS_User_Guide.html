Chapter 1: The Big Picture & How Omni Works

What is Omni HMOS?
Omni Hospitality Management Operating System (HMOS) is an all-in-one software platform that runs every single department of a modern luxury hotel or resort. Instead of using 10 different fragmented software tools for front desk, dining, housekeeping, kitchen, and accounting, Omni unites them into one single real-time brain.

How Everything Connects in Real-Time

When an action happens anywhere in the hotel, the rest of the hotel updates instantaneously:

1
A Guest Books or Arrives Whether booked through Booking.com or walking in at the lobby, their reservation instantly appears on the Front Desk Arrivals Manifest and reserves a room on the Room Availability Board.
2
Guest Checks In & Master Folio Opens When Front Desk clicks Check In, a Unified Master Folio (digital bill) is born. The guest's room key is encoded, and digital check-in stores their signed arrival card.
3
Guest Enjoys Hotel Services (Dining, Spa, Transport) When the guest dines at the restaurant, the waiter enters their order on the Restaurant POS. Clicking Fire to Kitchen sends tickets directly to the chef's Kitchen Display Screen. When the bill comes, clicking Post to Room instantly charges it to their hotel room folio—no cash needed!
4
Housekeeping Keeps Rooms Immaculate When a guest checks out, the room automatically turns Dirty on the Housekeeping Board. Once cleaned and inspected, the housekeeper taps Mark Clean, instantly making it available for new arrivals.
5
Night Audit, Financials & Executive Visibility At midnight, the Night Audit Engine balances all day transactions, calculates total daily revenue, updates RevPAR/ADR, and feeds real-time yields to the Executive Dashboard and Shareholder Portal.

Chapter 2: Front Desk & Room Operations

The Front Desk is the heartbeat of hotel operations. Staff can view upcoming arrivals, process departures, take walk-in guests, and inspect rooms.

1. Front Desk Dashboard & Quick Actions
Front Desk / Concierge

Screen Purpose: The primary cockpit for daily lobby operations. Shows total in-house guests, pending arrivals, departing rooms, and today's expected occupancy percentage.

Key Buttons & How to Use Them:

  • New Walk-In Booking: Opens a guest registration window. Use this when a guest arrives at the desk without a prior booking. Enter their name, contact details, select an available room type, and confirm dates.
  • Check In: Appears next to expected guests. Clicking this confirms their arrival, assigns their physical room number, and opens their Master Folio.
  • Check Out: Appears for departing guests. Clicking this reviews their final bill balance. If the balance is zero, it marks the room as departed and notifies Housekeeping to clean.
  • View Folio: Instantly opens the guest's billing statement to review charges, split invoices, or take payments.
2. Room Availability Board
Front Office / Housekeeping

Screen Purpose: A visual grid displaying every single room floor-by-floor (e.g., Deluxe King, Presidential Suite, Executive Suite) with color-coded status badges.

Badge Color Room Status What It Means in Plain English
Green Available & Clean Ready for immediate check-in. The room is inspected and vacant.
Blue Occupied A guest is currently staying in this room.
Orange Dirty / Turnover Guest checked out or requested cleaning. Housekeeping is assigned.
Red Out of Order (OOO) Room is temporarily offline due to maintenance or renovation.

Key Actions: Clicking any room opens its details—allowing front desk to change room rates, mark a room for maintenance, or change room assignment for a VIP guest.

3. Digital Contactless Check-In
Self-Service / Tablet

Screen Purpose: An iPad or mobile-friendly screen where guests can self-check-in at lobby kiosks or on their smartphones before arriving.

How It Works:

  • The guest enters their Confirmation Number or Booking Reference.
  • They verify their identity and can capture a photo of their passport or government ID.
  • They sign the digital registration card on the touch screen with their finger.
  • Clicking Complete Digital Check-In completes the check-in and dispatches their mobile key or instructs them to collect keycards at the express kiosk.

Chapter 3: Food & Beverage — Restaurant POS & Kitchen

Omni includes a high-speed Food & Beverage Point of Sale (POS) and an interactive Kitchen Display System (KDS) designed for restaurants, rooftop bars, and in-room dining.

1. Restaurant POS (Point of Sale)
Waiters / Bartenders / Cashiers

Screen Purpose: Taking dining orders, selecting menu items, managing table seatings, firing food orders to kitchen stations, and settling bills.

Step-by-Step Staff Workflow:

1
Select a Table: Click on an open dining table (e.g., Table 4, Patio 2, Pool Cabana).
2
Add Menu Items: Tap menu categories (Starters, Steaks, Pasta, Cocktails) and click items to add them to the Order Pad.
3
Click Fire to Kitchen: This instantly transmits a Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) to the chefs' display screens. Waiters do not have to walk into the kitchen!
4
Bill Settlement Options:
  • Post to Room: Enter the guest's room number (e.g., Room 304). The meal total is transferred directly onto their master hotel bill.
  • Settle Check: Use this for walk-in restaurant patrons paying by Credit Card, Debit Card, or Cash. Records payment and prints their tax receipt.
  • Print Check: Prints an itemized dining slip to present to the table before payment.
2. Kitchen Display System (KDS)
Head Chef / Line Cooks / Expediters

Screen Purpose: Mounted on kitchen touchscreens. Replaces noisy paper printers with live, color-coded electronic tickets that update over real-time WebSockets.

Ticket Flow & Status Bumping:

  • New Order (White/Blue): The ticket appears instantly with time elapsed (e.g., "Fired 2 mins ago"). Contains dishes, quantities, and cooking notes (e.g., "Medium Rare, dressing on side").
  • Start Prep: The line cook taps this when putting the steaks on the grill. Status turns to "Preparing".
  • Ready for Service: When plating is finished, cook taps this. Notifies service staff that Table 4 is ready to be served.
  • Bump / Served: Removes the ticket from active kitchen view once the food leaves the kitchen pass.

Chapter 4: Guest Billing & Unified Master Folios

Every resident guest has a Master Folio. In Omni, this folio acts as an itemized digital ledger that consolidates room charges, restaurant meals, spa sessions, minibar usage, and laundry into one clear bill.

Unified Folio Management
Front Desk / Cashier / Night Audit

What Staff See on the Folio Screen:

  • Folio Summary: Total Charges, Total Payments Received, and Outstanding Balance.
  • Itemized Ledger Table: Date, Department (e.g., "Room Night", "La Pergola Dining", "Spa Swedish Massage"), Reference ID, and Amount.

Key Buttons & Operations:

Button / Action What It Does & When to Use It
Process Payment Takes payment against the balance via Card, Cash, UPI, or Corporate Invoice. Immediately generates a payment receipt with transaction ID.
Add Manual Charge Manually posts a charge onto the guest's folio (e.g., lost keycard fee, airport transfer, minibar refill).
Split Folio Splits the bill between two guests sharing a room, or splits room charges to a Corporate Company Account and personal dining charges to the guest's personal credit card.
Print / Email Invoice Generates a luxury PDF tax invoice with VAT, GST, and property header to email directly to the guest or hand over upon checkout.
Post Adjustment / Void Applies a credit or discount if there was a billing dispute or complimentary courtesy agreed by the General Manager.

Chapter 5: Housekeeping Turnover & Engineering Maintenance

A hotel's reputation relies on spotless cleanliness and flawless room maintenance. Omni provides real-time digital custody of every room.

1. Housekeeping Turnover Hub
Executive Housekeeper / Room Attendants

Screen Purpose: Manages daily room cleaning rosters, tracks room turnover times, and ensures quality checklists are completed before guests check in.

Key Actions:

  • Assign Attendant: Assigns specific room floors (e.g., Floor 3: Rooms 301–315) to a housekeeper.
  • Start Cleaning: Attendant taps this upon entering the room. System tracks cleaning duration.
  • Mark Clean & Inspected: Supervisor checks linens, amenities, and cleanliness, then taps this. The room immediately switches to "Clean" on the Front Desk board.
  • Request Linen / Amenities: Alerts storehouse that extra towels or shampoo bottles are needed.
2. Engineering & Maintenance Work Orders
Maintenance Engineers / Facilities

Screen Purpose: Reporting and resolving facility issues (e.g., AC not cooling, plumbing leak, TV remote replacement) across guest rooms, banquet halls, and public spaces.

Key Actions:

  • Log Work Order: Staff creates a maintenance ticket. Select Room/Area, Issue Category, and Priority (Low, Medium, Critical Emergency).
  • Assign Technician: Dispatches duty engineer with required parts.
  • Complete & Resolve: Engineer closes ticket after testing equipment. If the room was marked "Out of Order", resolving the ticket automatically restores it to service.

Events Director / Sales

Purpose: Booking massage therapies, facials, and wellness sessions with dedicated therapists and treatment suites.

  • Book Appointment: Selects treatment, therapist, and available room slot.

Purpose: Scheduling airport VIP pickups, limousine rentals, and guest city tours.

  • Schedule Airport Transfer: Records flight number, terminal, pickup time, and assigns a hotel chauffeur and luxury vehicle.

Chapter 7: Inventory Audits & Financial Control

1. Central Storehouse Inventory & Stock Audits
Storekeeper / Procurement Manager

Purpose: Managing hotel supplies (beverages, food staples, bathroom amenities, cleaning chemicals) and preventing stockouts.

Key Operations:

  • Par Level Tracking: System highlights items in red when current quantity dips below safety thresholds.
  • Create Purchase Order (PO): Generates an official purchase requisition to order replenishment goods from approved vendors.
  • Start Stock Count (Physical Audit): Opens the Stock Audit Tool. Storekeepers walk through the warehouse, type in the physical count, and the system automatically calculates the variance (surplus or shortage) and commits audited stock adjustments.
2. Finance & End-of-Day Night Audit
Financial Controller / Night Auditor

Purpose: The official close of the hotel business day. Reconciles cash, credit cards, ledger charges, and posts daily room and tax charges onto every active guest folio.

Step-by-Step Night Audit Workflow:

1
Verify Cashier Drawers: Review daily cash and credit card receipts collected across Front Desk, Bar, and Restaurants.
2
Click Run Night Audit: The system locks the business date, automatically posts tonight's room rates + applicable taxes to all in-house folios, rolls the hotel operating date to tomorrow, and archives daily financial statements.

Chapter 8: Revenue, Executive & Shareholder Intelligence

1. Dynamic AI Pricing & Surge Engine
Revenue Manager / General Manager

Purpose: Maximizing hotel revenue by automatically adjusting room rates based on current occupancy, city events, weekend demand, and seasonal surges.

  • Enable AI Dynamic Pricing: When occupancy crosses 85%, room prices automatically increase by 20% to maximize profitability.
  • Set Surge Multiplier: Staff can apply instant holiday or festival surge rates across room categories with one click.
2. OTA Channel Manager (Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb)
E-Commerce & Distribution Manager

Purpose: Keeping hotel inventory synchronized across global travel websites in real time to prevent overbooking.

  • Sync All Channels: Pushes live rates and remaining room counts to external OTAs.
  • Two-Way Instant Booking: When someone reserves on Expedia, that room is instantly blocked in Omni, avoiding double bookings.
3. Executive KPI Dashboard
General Manager / C-Level Executives

Purpose: High-level business cockpit tracking key industry health indicators:

  • RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room): Total room revenue divided by total rooms in the hotel.
  • ADR (Average Daily Rate): The average rental price realized per paid occupied room.
  • Occupancy Rate (%): Percentage of available rooms currently filled.
  • GOPPAR (Gross Operating Profit Per Available Room): True operational bottom-line health.
4. Shareholder & Investor Portal
Hotel Owners / Equity Investors

Purpose: A dedicated, secure dashboard for property owners and equity shareholders to review their investment performance.

  • Cap Table Breakdown: Shows equity percentage and shareholder shares.
  • Dividend Yield: Displays annualized return on investment.
  • Download Distribution Statement: Generates official quarterly dividend payout slips.

Summary: 5 Golden Rules for Hotel Staff

  1. Always Post to Room with Confirmation: When dining guests charge meals to their room, verify the room number and guest surname on the screen before confirming.
  2. Mark Rooms Promptly: Housekeepers should tap Mark Clean immediately after finishing an inspection so Front Desk can check in waiting guests without delays.
  3. Conduct Weekly Stock Audits: Use the Start Stock Count tool weekly in the central pantry to catch discrepancies early.
  4. Perform Night Audit on Time: Run the Night Audit daily between 1:00 AM and 3:00 AM once all late restaurant checks have been settled.
  5. Keep Guest Notes Updated: Add guest dietary preferences (e.g., "Gluten-Free") and room preferences (e.g., "High Floor, extra pillows") in the Guest CRM to ensure unforgettable luxury hospitality!
